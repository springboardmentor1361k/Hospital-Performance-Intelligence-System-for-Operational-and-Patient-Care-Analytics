MedTrack_DV – KPI Definitions

1. Total Admissions

Definition:
The total number of unique hospital admissions.

Formula:
COUNTD(admission_id)

Source Dataset:
Hospital Overview Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
500 admissions

Interpretation:
This KPI shows the total number of patients admitted to the hospital during the available data period.


2. Average Length of Stay (LOS)

Definition:
The average number of days patients stayed in the hospital.

Formula:
Average of length_of_stay_days

Source Dataset:
Hospital Overview Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
4.98 days

Interpretation:
A higher average LOS indicates that patients are staying longer in the hospital on average.


3. Readmission Rate

Definition:
The percentage of hospital admissions marked as readmissions.

Formula:
(Readmitted Admissions / Total Unique Admissions) × 100

Source Dataset:
Hospital Overview Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
13.00%

Readmission Count:
65

Denominator:
500 unique admissions

Interpretation:
This KPI indicates the proportion of admissions that were identified as readmissions.


4. Mortality Rate

Definition:
The percentage of hospital admissions associated with a mortality flag.

Formula:
(Mortality Count / Total Unique Admissions) × 100

Source Dataset:
Hospital Overview Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
1.40%

Mortality Count:
7

Denominator:
500 unique admissions

Interpretation:
This KPI represents the percentage of admissions associated with a mortality flag in the dataset.



5. Bed Occupancy Rate

Definition:
The percentage of available department beds represented by occupied beds across the department-level records.

Formula:
(Total Occupied Beds / Total Available Beds) × 100

Source Dataset:
Department Analytics Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
69.66%

Total Occupied Beds:
11,946

Total Available Beds:
17,150

Interpretation:
This KPI indicates how much of the available bed capacity is occupied across the department-level records.


6. Bed Utilization Rate

Definition:
The percentage of available beds that are recorded as being in use in the Resource Utilization Dataset.

Formula:
(Total Beds in Use / Total Beds Available) × 100

Source Dataset:
Resource Utilization Dataset

Current Dataset Result:
62.63%

Total Beds in Use:
3,077

Total Beds Available:
4,913

Interpretation:
This KPI indicates the utilization of beds recorded in the resource utilization data.

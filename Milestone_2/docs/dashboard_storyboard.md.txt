MedTrack_DV – Dashboard Storyboard

 1. Hospital Overview Dashboard
Purpose
Provide an overall view of hospital operations, admissions, patient outcomes, length of stay, and bed utilization.

KPIs

* Total Admissions
* Average Length of Stay
* Readmission Rate
* Mortality Rate
* Bed Occupancy Rate
* Bed Utilization Rate

Charts

* Monthly Admissions Trend – Line Chart
* Admissions vs Discharges – Line/Bar Chart
* Admissions by Department – Bar Chart
* Admissions by Admission Type – Bar Chart
* Hospital Performance Overview – KPI Cards

 Filters

* Hospital
* Department
* Date
* Year
* Month

 Interactions

* Selecting a department filters the related information.
* Selecting a time period updates the KPI cards and charts.
* Navigation button connects to Patient Flow, Department Analytics, and Resource Utilization dashboards.

 2. Patient Flow Dashboard
Purpose
Analyze patient movement through departments, transfers, department stay duration, and peak patient movement periods.

 KPIs

* Total Admissions
* Total Discharges
* Total Transfers
* Average Department Stay

 Charts

* Patient Movement by Department – Bar Chart
* Patient Movement Over Time – Line Chart
* Movement Type Distribution – Bar/Pie Chart
* Peak Movement Hours – Heatmap
* Average Stay by Department – Bar Chart

 Filters

* Hospital
* Department
* Date
* Year
* Month
* Shift
* Movement Type

 Interactions

* Selecting a department shows its patient movement details.
* Selecting a time period updates movement trends.
* Navigation connects to Department Analytics and Resource Utilization.

 3. Department Analytics Dashboard
Purpose

Compare department performance using admissions, discharges, occupancy, length of stay, staffing, and efficiency indicators.

KPIs

* Patients Admitted
* Patients Discharged
* Bed Occupancy Rate
* Average Length of Stay
* Readmission Rate
* Staff-to-Patient Ratio
* Department Efficiency Score

Charts

* Admissions and Discharges by Department – Bar Chart
* Bed Occupancy by Department – Bar Chart
* Average Length of Stay by Department – Bar Chart
* Capacity vs Demand – Bar Chart
* Department Efficiency Score – Bar Chart
* Staff-to-Patient Ratio – Bar Chart

Filters

* Hospital
* Department
* Date
* Year
* Month

Interactions

* Selecting a department highlights its performance.
* Selecting a department can navigate to Patient Flow.
* Selecting a department can navigate to Resource Utilization.

4. Resource Utilization Dashboard
Purpose
Analyze utilization of hospital beds, equipment, and clinical staff, including shortages, maintenance, downtime, and overtime.

KPIs

* Bed Utilization Rate
* Units in Use
* Units Under Maintenance
* Equipment Downtime
* Overtime Hours
* Nurses on Duty
* Doctors on Duty

Charts

* Bed Utilization – Bar Chart
* Equipment Utilization – Bar Chart
* Resource Usage by Department – Bar Chart
* Equipment Downtime – Bar Chart
* Resource Shortage Status – Bar/Indicator Chart
* Staff Overtime – Bar Chart

Filters

* Hospital
* Department
* Date
* Year
* Month
* Resource Type
* Resource Category

Interactions

* Selecting a resource type filters the related resource information.
* Selecting a department shows department-specific resource utilization.
* Navigation connects back to Hospital Overview and Department Analytics.

Dashboard Interlinking

The four dashboards will be interconnected using Tableau navigation and dashboard actions.

Hospital Overview
↓
Patient Flow
↓
Department Analytics
↓
Resource Utilization

Common filters:

* Hospital
* Department
* Date
* Year
* Month

Dashboard interactions will allow users to select a department or time period and explore related operational information across dashboards.

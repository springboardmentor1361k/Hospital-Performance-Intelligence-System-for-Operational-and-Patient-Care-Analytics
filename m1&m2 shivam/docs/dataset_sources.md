1. Hospital HMIS Dataset for Healthcare Analytics 
link - https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shalakagangurde/hospital-hmis-dataset-for-healthcare-analytics?utm_source=chatgpt.com

This dataset represents a synthetic but realistic Hospital Management Information System (HMIS) designed for healthcare analytics, dashboards, and operational insights.

It models how a real hospital stores and manages data related to:

Patient admissions
Diseases and diagnostics
Billing and insurance
Pharmacy and inventory
Staff management (doctors, nurses, technicians, admins)
The data spans multiple years (2020–2025) and follows a normalized relational database design, closely resembling real-world hospital information systems.

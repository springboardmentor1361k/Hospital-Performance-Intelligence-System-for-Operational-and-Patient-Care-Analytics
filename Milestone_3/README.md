# Milestone 3: Tableau Analytical Dashboards & Department Intelligence

## Overview
Milestone 3 implements the interactive business intelligence dashboard suite for the **MedTrack Hospital Performance Intelligence System**, covering executive situational awareness, patient clinical flow, and resource utilization.

## Delivered Dashboards

1. **Hospital Overview**: Institutional admissions, bed occupancy, ALOS against the 7-day CMS target, weekly arrival trends, and patient satisfaction benchmarks.
2. **Patient Flow & Quality Analysis**: Inpatient movement velocity (admissions vs. discharges), stay distributions, age demographic breakdown, and surge event impact.
3. **Resource & Utilization Dashboard**: Bed capacity utilization against the 85% safety threshold, clinical workforce stress indices, and nurse/physician saturation heatmaps.
